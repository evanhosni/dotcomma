# Lighting

## How it works

- **Day/night** ([dayNight.ts](dayNight.ts)): module-level channels written every frame by [world/sky/DayNightCycle.tsx](../world/sky/DayNightCycle.tsx) from the server clock via `nightBlendAt`. Read them from `useFrame`:
  - `getNightBlend()` — day-to-night blend;
  - `getWindowLightsProgress()` — drives window lights and lamp glow;
  - `getNightIndex()` — changes each nightfall so different windows light;
  - `NIGHT_BLEND_UNIFORM` + `nightDimGLSL()` (darkens by `NIGHT_GROUND_DIM`) for unlit shaders. The tunnel programs do NOT call it: `tunnelShade` (world/shaders/tunnelRock.ts) dims only its daylit term by the same amount, so a tunnel's deep ambient and the personal light are the same by day and by night.
- **Scene lights** ([DayNightLights.tsx](DayNightLights.tsx)): a hemisphere light (cool sky above, warm ground bounce below — so up, side and down faces differ), a key directional that swings from sun to moon, and a dim low fill from off the sun's opposite azimuth (so the two shadow-side walls differ); by night the hemisphere turns neutral and the fill goes out. All dimmed by the blend. Only lit materials see them.
- **Personal light** ([personalLight.ts](personalLight.ts)): the local player's own faint light in the dark — a term of the tunnel shaders around `cameraPosition` ([world/shaders/tunnelRock.ts](../world/shaders/tunnelRock.ts) `tunnelShade`, scaled by the one `uPersonalLight` uniform), never a scene light, so no other client ever sees it. The Player drives it every frame (`drivePersonalLight(daylight, dt)`) from the daylight at its eyes (`entranceDaylightAt`, the underground registry): after 1s of COMPLETE darkness (daylight under 0.08) it fades in over 2s, and out over 0.6s once the daylight passes 0.22 (hysteresis, so a spot at the edge of the dark never blinks it). It reaches 1.5–10u at 22% of the surface color. The devmode FLASHLIGHT (`DEV_TOGGLES` `flashlight`): `driveFlashlight(on, direction, dt)` from the Player every frame; a cone (~18° full, gone at ~35°, 15–60u) along the camera's view in the same tunnel shaders, through `uFlashlight` / `uFlashlightDir`. Both are in `PERSONAL_LIGHT_UNIFORMS`. Neither reaches the terrain or the dressing outside: the tunnels are where it is dark.
- **Tunnel light on actors** ([tunnelLight.ts](tunnelLight.ts), applied by `prepareActorMaterial`): an actor's vertex in a tunnel's AIR — inside one of the skirt masks the terrain already receives (the 32 nearest), under its `ceiling` — is lit by THE tunnel light (world/shaders/tunnelRock.ts `tunnelLightFactor`, the walls' own: the deep ambient, the personal light and flashlight, the daylight of an opening near it) instead of the scene's sun, moon and ambient, so the day/night cycle reaches it only where it reaches the rock beside it. Per vertex, after skinning; positional (no per-object state); lit materials only. Shadows were the alternative: a shadow map blocks only the directional light (the ambient would still light a beeble in a sewer), costs a scene render a frame and new programs, and the terrain draws its own fake sun (it would not cast).
- **Lamp glow** ([lampGlow.ts](lampGlow.ts)): street lamps and signals light the ground with no real lights.
  - Each source is a `LampHead` (position + index into `LAMP_COLORS`) in `activeLampHeads`.
  - The heads are periodically written into a camera-centered grid texture of `LAMP_CELL_SIZE` cells, one head per cell.
  - Shaders sample their neighboring cells (`lampGlowAccumGLSL`) with falloff `LAMP_GLOW_RADIUS`, so cost is constant for any lamp count.
  - A registered head fades in over `LAMP_FADE_SECONDS` and a released one fades out before it is deleted (the fade rides in the texel's w), so a dressing chunk mounting or dropping at the render distance never switches a block's light at once. The grid is rewritten every few frames while anything fades.
- **City lights** ([world/…/city/lights.tsx](../world/domains/overworld/regions/city/biomes/city/lights.tsx)): a fixed pool of point lights on the nearest city sites (6, fading toward `scanRadius`), and a larger fixed pool of AURA sprites (16) reaching as far as the buildings' far sprites (`auraDistance` = `BUILDING_SPRITE_LOD.renderDistance`, fading over its last 25%): a far city's glow reads at night though its light would light nothing. Each slot fades out as the next site outside the pool closes in (`RANK_FADE_BAND`) and toward the scan's edge, so re-picks never switch a light; each aura sprite also fades as the camera approaches its view plane (`AURA_NEAR_HIDE`/`SHOW`) — a screen-aligned sprite otherwise vanished whole as the camera passed it.
  - `LampGlowDriver` (in [world/CustomCanvas.tsx](../world/CustomCanvas.tsx)) runs `driveLampLighting`; intensity follows `getWindowLightsProgress()`.
  - Receivers: terrain, every actor material (`patchStandardMaterialLampGlow` via `prepareActorMaterial`), bridge decks.

## How to add another

- **A program the personal light reaches**: include `TUNNEL_ROCK_GLSL` (it declares the uniform) and spread `PERSONAL_LIGHT_UNIFORMS` into the material's uniforms; call `tunnelShade` with the surface's position from the camera (`vWorldPosAbs - cameraPosition`).
- **Glow source**: call `registerLampHeads("<key>", heads)` when the heads exist and call its returned disposer when they go away (see `street-lamps`). Recolor with `setLampHeadColor`.
- **Glow color**: append `{ name, rgb }` to `LAMP_COLORS` (never reorder), and optionally `export const LAMP_COLOR_<NAME> = lampColorIndex("<name>")`.
- **Glow receiver**:
  - `MeshStandardMaterial`: `patchStandardMaterialLampGlow(material)`.
  - `ShaderMaterial`: spread `LAMP_GRID_UNIFORMS` into its uniforms, add `LAMP_GLOW_UNIFORMS_GLSL` to the fragment declarations, use `lampGlowAccumGLSL("<absolute world pos>")` and add `lampGlowSum` (see [bridges/material.ts](../objects/dressing/bridges/material.ts)).
