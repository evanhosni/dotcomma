# Generation workers and the height pipeline

## How it works

- [index.ts](index.ts) is the ONLY height implementation. After `initCompute(domainConfig)`, `computeVertexData(x, z)` returns a `VertexResult` ([types.ts](types.ts)): height plus blend fields, road/river distances and water height. The terrain, spawn, foliage and dressing workers, the main thread and the server all run it. It is Three-free and re-exports every module's public API, so outside callers import from it only. Step order is traced in `world/VERTEX_JOURNEY.md`.
- Results share scratch buffers and some steps recurse: copy what you keep. Modules import each other in cycles, so values cross modules only inside functions. A folder's `constants.ts` (and `roads/utils.ts`, `roads/city/utils.ts`) is a LEAF: it imports nothing from the pipeline but the live config ([store.ts](store.ts)), so a top-level value may read it.

**Modules**
- [zones/index.ts](zones/index.ts): region and biome grids, sites, rolls, walls (`getZoneWalls`, `getBiomeContext`) and places (`getPlaceInfo`, `findRegionCell`, `findBiomeCell`). [zones/blend/index.ts](zones/blend/index.ts): the wall pass (`accumulateWallFields`, `combineZoneWeights`) and shader slots (`biomeSlotsOf`, `combineSlotWeights`).
- [utils.ts](utils.ts): `terrainNoise`, `biomeNoiseHeight`, road warp `warp`/`unwarp`/`warpMax`. [cache/](cache): `CellCache`, `PointCache` (a class file each), `dropOldestHalf` ([utils.ts](cache/utils.ts)).
- [water/](water): WATER in the pipeline, one folder per body under its base (decisions: [water/DECISIONS.md](water/DECISIONS.md); the client side: [world/water](../world/water/README.md)). The BASE: [index.ts](water/index.ts) is step 4, `resolveWater(ctx: WaterContext)` — the ONE composition of a vertex's water (area, channel, walls, joins, owners); [channel/](water/channel/index.ts) the carve every river and pond shares (the channel, its walls, its `<Banks/>` relief, all blended by zone weight; `WALL_DEFAULTS`, `BANK_DEFAULTS` in its params.ts); [joins/](water/joins/index.ts) where a river meets a lake (mouth, the hold beside quays, merge, the widening toward lakes); [utils.ts](water/utils.ts) what the water reads of the world — the ground it hangs from (`waterGroundAt`, `keepingVertex`) and the ONE door to roads/, bridges/ and the city; [params.ts](water/params.ts) the base's kinds, `WATER_DEFAULTS` (`fallGrade`), `waterBodyDefaults` (`<Water defaults>`), `keepsOwnBanks` (`<Quays/>`) and the body slots (`bodyOf`); [registry.ts](water/registry.ts) the body kinds (`registerWaterBodyKind`); [material.ts](water/material.ts) the colours (`writeWaterColors`); [types.ts](water/types.ts), [constants.ts](water/constants.ts). The BODIES: [lakes/](water/lakes/index.ts) (level, bowl, shore lift; `lakeOf` in its params.ts), [rivers/](water/rivers) (below), [ponds/](water/ponds/index.ts) (the standalone ponds, every pond's kind roll, spot and disc). The PARTS: [parts/](water/parts/index.ts) (THE ZONE RULE, `waterZoneOf`, in its params.ts; the one scatter for bubbles, steam and an `<Actor>` in a body; [waterfall-boulders/](water/parts/waterfall-boulders/index.ts) and [waterfall-splashes/](water/parts/waterfall-splashes/index.ts), the `<Water>` base's falls).
- [water/rivers/](water/rivers): rivers are the edges of a third voronoi grid; the rest of water/ reads them through [index.ts](water/rivers/index.ts) (the pipeline's other systems import the river modules directly). [network/](water/rivers/network/index.ts) (`getRiverSegments`, the piece API) rolls edges by `<Rivers chance>`, sizes each junction (`<Rivers width>`, the lakes' widening), shapes every river end (a pond where a `<Pond riverEnd>` rolls, else round; a mouth; a road's blunt end) and gives each joint's water (`edgeSurfaces`); [gaps/](water/rivers/gaps/index.ts) (`ownCodes`, `gorgedCodes`, the one junction gap finder and `riseOverWaterLine`) decides which pieces are built; [ponds/](water/rivers/ponds/index.ts) (`climbedCodes`) climbs the gaps the gorges left to a pond on top where a `<Pond summit>` rolls, and holds the ponds a river holds at its ends and mid-river (`riverEndPondOf`: an end's one roll); [road-layer/](water/rivers/road-layer/index.ts) (`roadCodes`, `finalCodes`) drops river where a road follows it or no deck may carry a road over it; [field/](water/rivers/field/index.ts) (`riverFieldAt`, `riverQuayAt`) the per-vertex distance, width, surface, flow and walls; [bed-limit/](water/rivers/bed-limit/index.ts) (`capRiverBed`) where the bed paint ends on a biome whose riverbed yields; [params.ts](water/rivers/params.ts) the `<Rivers>` props and the gap rules (`riversIn(zone)`: biome → region → `<Water defaults>` → `RIVERS_DEFAULTS`; an edge carries its midpoint's as `RiverEdge.rules`). Shared numbers and reaches (`riverKeepOff`, `riverWetReach`) are in [constants.ts](water/rivers/constants.ts), the `PIECE_*` codes in [enums.ts](water/rivers/enums.ts), shapes in [types.ts](water/rivers/types.ts).
- [roads/](roads): `getNetwork` ([freeways/network/](roads/freeways/network/index.ts)) links cities within `FREEWAY_LINK_CELLS` by shortest wall paths into `FreewayRun`s, graded and laid off the city (step 5) by [freeways/index.ts](roads/freeways/index.ts) (`freewayGradeAt`, `gradeOffCityFreeway`). The city: `getCityTerrain` ([city/index.ts](roads/city/index.ts), which also holds the block cells and remnants and the belt or its waterfront) turns the districts and arterials ([city/utils.ts](roads/city/utils.ts)) and the quays into one road field and plateau height; the field's shared formulas (`freewayField`, `cityCurbDip`) are in [roads/utils.ts](roads/utils.ts), `NO_ROAD_DISTANCE` in [roads/constants.ts](roads/constants.ts). The dressing enumerators (road markers, traffic lights, freeway side points, city sites, run lamps) are all in [dressing/index.ts](roads/dressing/index.ts); the city ones walk a chunk through [dressing/utils.ts](roads/dressing/utils.ts). [fragments/](roads/fragments/index.ts) turns river-severed road scraps into bank, removes city block islands too small to build on (to road or to bank), and removes bare road specks away from deck ends.
- [bridges/](bridges): `getFreewayBridges` ([bridges/index.ts](bridges/index.ts)); the ground under decks is [ground/index.ts](bridges/ground/index.ts) over [deck/drawn-slab/](bridges/deck/drawn-slab/index.ts) and [ground/utils.ts](bridges/ground/utils.ts) (a landed end's approach and mouth); see [Bridges](../objects/dressing/bridges/README.md).
- [placement/flatten-pads/](placement/flatten-pads/index.ts): `flattenGround` actors get a deterministic pad under each instance; `getFlattenPoints` feeds both the spawn worker and `applyFlattenPads`. `computeVertexDataRaw` is the pad-free height.
- [placement/utils.ts](placement/utils.ts): the one density roll and placement filters (incl. `riverKeepOff`). [placement/index.ts](placement/index.ts): `generateDensityPoints`. [placement/registry.ts](placement/registry.ts): a feature's own placements (`featurePlacesIn`).
- [underground/](underground): the tunnels (caves and sewers under the biomes whose spec's `features` mount `<Caves>` / `<Sewers>`) as data every thread agrees on — [underground/index.ts](underground/index.ts) (one system per biome cell: `systemAt`, `systemsNear`, `caveMouthsNear`, `generatingTunnels`; and the ONE height step they make, the mouth bulge `mouthBulgeAt` — step 10, from mouths fetched at step 0; nothing under water, faded out near roads, freeways and rivers — with the growth keep-off), `underground/caves/index.ts` (on `underground/caves/utils.ts`; caves hold no water), `underground/sewers/index.ts` (+ `underground/sewers/geometry.ts`, `underground/sewers/utils.ts` the districts), `underground/field/index.ts` (the air as one signed field, and a mouth's geometry; `underground/field/TunnelSpace.ts`), `underground/caves/geometry.ts` (the surface nets and the entrance patch's buffers), [drawn-ground/](drawn-ground/index.ts) (the ground as LOD1 draws it), `underground/chunks/index.ts` (THE entry the tunnel worker and the server build chunks by, `buildUndergroundChunk`: `buildCaveChunk` and `buildSewerChunk`, both on `underground/chunks/ChunkAssembly.ts` and `underground/chunks/utils.ts` — the vertex, the one daylight rule, the mesh and column colliders; the collider bodies in `underground/chunks/spec.ts`). Decisions: [underground/DECISIONS.md](underground/DECISIONS.md); the client: [world/underground](../world/underground/README.md).
- [snow/](snow): where snow lies and how deep over `snow` biomes — ONE rule in [snow/index.ts](snow/index.ts) (the cover, the amount and depth, the terrain's whitening at every LOD `terrainSnowDepth`), read in the same file by the near sheet (`getSnowDrift`) and the wading (`snowMovementAt`, the drag and traction every capsule gets through `surfaceModifiersAt`); its defaults in [snow/params.ts](snow/params.ts). [rocks/index.ts](rocks/index.ts): `getRocks`, the rock dressing's placement (a density field per `rocks` biome kept by slope, plus every cave mouth's spots; defaults in [rocks/params.ts](rocks/params.ts)). The water's particle emitters and boulders are its parts ([water/parts/](water/parts/index.ts)).
- [registry.ts](registry.ts): WHAT A BIOME CARRIES, generically. A biome's nature is whatever components are mounted inside its `<Biome>`: their resolved params land in `SerializedBiome.features` under each declaration's kind, and `biomeIdsWith(kind)`, `biomeFeature(id, kind)` and `featureMounts(kind)` read them from the LIVE config. This module knows no kind by name — each system exports its own typed accessors beside its component (`snowBiomeIds()` in `snow/params.ts`, `caveBiomeIds()`/`sewerBiomeIds()` in `underground/params.ts`, `rockFieldBiomeIds()` in `rocks/params.ts`, `lakeBiomeIds()` in `water/lakes/params.ts`), so deleting a system deletes every mention of it. Never a biome id or name in a system (the bespoke city's height branch is the one exception, and it is turned into "road-bearing" here).
- [utils/worker-client/index.ts](../utils/worker-client/index.ts): `createWorkerClient` (lazy boot, INIT, id-matched requests, `reset()`). The threads that run this pipeline each live beside their client, outside this folder: [terrain](../world/terrain/terrain.worker.ts), [spawn](../objects/actors/spawning/spawn.worker.ts), [foliage](../objects/foliage/foliage.worker.ts), [dressing](../objects/dressing/dressing.worker.ts), [underground](../world/underground/underground.worker.ts) (the tunnel worker: its own thread, a chunk is 100–400ms; it always answers).

## How to add another

- **Noise-height biome:** nothing here; mount a `<Noise>` in its biome component.
- **Bespoke-height biome:**
  1. Add its id to `src/world/constants.ts` and mount no `<Noise>` in it.
  2. Write a pure height module here, a feature folder with its `index.ts` (like [roads/city/index.ts](roads/city/index.ts)); clear its caches in `initCompute`.
  3. Branch on the id in `zoneBiomeHeight` ([index.ts](index.ts)), scaled by presence.
  4. Add a case to [tests/blend.test.ts](tests/blend.test.ts).
- **Flatten-pad actor:** set `flattenGround: true` on its `ActorSpec` (like `objects/actors/building/spec.ts`) or its `<Actor>` mount.
- **Dressing enumerator:**
  1. Write `getMyPoints(minX, minZ, maxX, maxZ, …)` in its feature's `index.ts` (like `getCityTrafficLightPoints` in [roads/dressing/index.ts](roads/dressing/index.ts)), owning each point by its chunk and validating via `computeVertexData`; re-export it from [index.ts](index.ts).
  2. Add one entry to `DRESSING_ENUMERATORS` in `src/objects/dressing/registry.ts`.
  3. Add determinism and chunk-split cases to [roads/tests/cityFeatures.test.ts](roads/tests/cityFeatures.test.ts).
- **River rule** (whether a piece is built): one more verdict in `classify` or one more pass in `ownCodes` ([water/rivers/gaps/index.ts](water/rivers/gaps/index.ts)), with a `PIECE_*` code in [water/rivers/enums.ts](water/rivers/enums.ts) if it needs its own reason; a rule the roads impose goes in [water/rivers/road-layer/index.ts](water/rivers/road-layer/index.ts). Add a case to [water/rivers/tests/banks.test.ts](water/rivers/tests/banks.test.ts) or [water/rivers/gaps/tests/gorges.test.ts](water/rivers/gaps/tests/gorges.test.ts).
