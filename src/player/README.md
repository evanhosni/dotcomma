# Player

## How it works

[Player.tsx](Player.tsx) is a kinematic capsule (size in [spec.ts](spec.ts): `PLAYER_HEIGHT`, `PLAYER_RADIUS`) under pointer-lock controls, drawn as a beeble, mounted once by `CustomCanvas` so it survives domain switches. Each frame:

1. **Input**: [player/hooks.ts](hooks.ts) keeps held keys (`InputState`, `KEY_BINDINGS`); movement follows the camera's flattened axes.
2. **Hold at spawn**: while `terrainLoaded` is false the capsule is pinned `SPAWN_DROP_HEIGHT` (100u) above `playerSpawn` (GameContext), then falls once the ground exists — so a spawn inside a building lands on it, never inside it. This is how domain switches and fast travel move the player.
3. **Move**: noclip flies freely; otherwise `stepCharacter` from [physics/index.ts](../physics/index.ts) resolves all movement at `WALK_SPEED` / `SPRINT_SPEED`. [player/hooks.ts](hooks.ts) samples the water surface under the player every few frames (the same pipeline that draws it, on the dressing worker) and passes it as the step's `swim` input: once `SWIM_SUBMERGED_FRACTION` of the capsule is under, the player sinks slowly, a held Space swims up and floats the head at the surface, and a fresh press with the head near the surface breaches out (`SWIM_*` in [physics/constants.ts](../physics/constants.ts)).
   The camera's depth under that surface drives the underwater pass ([vfx/screen-pass/UnderwaterPass.ts](../vfx/screen-pass/UnderwaterPass.ts), `setCameraWaterDepth`, with the liquid's look: a tunnel's sewage, or its water in the colours of the body the probe sampled).
   **Underground** ([player/hooks.ts](hooks.ts), `useUndergroundMode`, run before the step): whether the player is in a cave or sewer and what the terrain heightfields are to it there (the step's `ignoreTerrain`: overhead terrain is not ground), plus the manholes' catch, drop, climb (`vyOverride`) and exit. Inside, the tunnels' green sewage is the liquid. How the tunnels work: [world/underground](../world/underground/README.md).
4. **Safety nets** ([player/hooks.ts](hooks.ts), `useGroundSafetyNets`) against the analytic terrain height: a periodic backstop that lifts the capsule if it is below the surface, a stuck escape, and a fall reset. The backstop and the stuck escape are suspended while the player is in a tunnel (under the ground on purpose). In a tunnel, `player/hooks.ts` has its own two: a capsule whose axis is in the rock beside it is pushed out along the tunnel field's gradient the same frame, and feet sunk under a floor are set back on the air over it (the underground README, "The player"). `resolveEmbeddedSurface` reads the PADDED height (pads excavate, so a raw one would lift a player out of a building's excavation). Every frequent sample, these and the water probe's, runs on the dressing worker (`getVertexSample`), one in flight each: on the main thread a new cell's first sample built its river list and road network, 20–70ms frames while flying (PERFORMANCE.md).
5. **Body**: [PlayerBody.tsx](PlayerBody.tsx) is a beeble clone from the actors' model pool, posed by the NPCs' AnimationChannel + AnimationPlayer. Each frame Player.tsx samples what the player did (`PlayerActionSample`) and `playerAnimationOf` ([utils.ts](utils.ts)) picks the clip from `PLAYER_CLIPS` ([constants.ts](constants.ts)): idle, walk (sped to the ground speed), run, jump, fall, slide, swim, tread water, climb, fly. A clip the GLB lacks logs `animation "<name>" does not exist` once and holds the last pose, so the console lists the clips still to make. The beeble spec's collider IS the player capsule, so every NPC beeble is the player's size.
6. **Camera** follows the capsule. The middle mouse button toggles third person (`useThirdPersonToggle`): the camera hangs `THIRD_PERSON_DISTANCE` behind the head, over a shoulder (`THIRD_PERSON_SHOULDER`, `THIRD_PERSON_RISE`) that leaves the body on the left of the screen by default (`setThirdPersonBodySide`, [store.ts](store.ts): the hook for a future settings-menu option), pulled in front of whatever a Rapier ray along the boom hits; the body turns to the walking direction and its head to the look. The boom's length along the look is published ([store.ts](store.ts)) and every crosshair pick (actors, doors, manholes, the CRT) starts its ray there through `aimFromCamera` ([utils.ts](utils.ts)), so reach is measured from the player and the player's own body never blocks a click. The stats overlay's Pos is that same point.
   `playerPosition` (GameContext) is updated for streaming, the address bar and networking ([net/players/LocalPlayerSync.tsx](../net/players/LocalPlayerSync.tsx)).

## How to add another

N/A — there is one global player.

Tune `WALK_SPEED` / `SPRINT_SPEED` ([Player.tsx](Player.tsx)), the capsule in [spec.ts](spec.ts), `CAMERA_FAR` ([constants.ts](constants.ts), sized with the terrain LODs), and key bindings in `KEY_BINDINGS` ([player/hooks.ts](hooks.ts)).
