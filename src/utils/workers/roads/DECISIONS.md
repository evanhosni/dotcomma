# Roads and the city — decisions

> Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md (Oct 2026) to keep it under the context limit. Section names quoted in the text ("Blending", "Rivers", …) refer to the other decision docs listed in CLAUDE.md under "Where the docs are".

## The wall network

**The wall NETWORK — freeways live on the biome walls** (`getNetwork` in `utils/workers/roads/freewayNetwork.ts`, cached per biome-grid cell). Over a WIDE window of sites (`NETWORK_RADIUS_CELLS` = 18 each way, its own Delaunay) it decides which walls carry a freeway, so every wall a vertex can see is decided from the same complete neighborhood in every window (`networkOf(ctx)` gives a query context its cell's network, built only when something asks — river surfaces, sky polls and zone lookups never need it). EVERY site of the window is zoned by its OWN region grid (`biomeSiteAt`): the window center's 5×5 region grid (±2 region cells, ~6000u) ends inside the window's 9000u reach and handed the outer sites whatever region was nearest within it (fixed Sept 2026; MEASURED over 10,000 windows in a 50×50 km square: every freeway wall in each window's own cell unchanged (916 of 916), and 356 of 183,173 window-wall pairs within 3500u of a window's center replaced by 805 others, in 330 of 9111 windows — the runs a window reports toward its edge, which the bridge scan reads). Nothing within `NETWORK_BORDER_CELLS` (3) of the window's edge is trusted (the Delaunay is distorted there): hops and whole paths there are dropped. (Rivers used to be traced along biome walls here — the next paragraph says why they left.)

## City terrain

**City terrain** (`utils/workers/roads/cityTerrain.ts`, tuned via `cityConfig` in `world/defaults.ts`): the city is partitioned into large STAGGERED DISTRICTS (jittered rows ~`districtSize` cells tall split into staggered jittered segments — sizes ~0.6–1.4×; `cityDistrictByIndex`), and EACH DISTRICT ROTATES its entire block grid by a seeded multiple of 15° (15°–75°; 0° is deliberately excluded so every district reads as rotated) about its own center (`getCityTerrain` transforms the vertex into the district-local frame; distances/heights are rotation-invariant so nothing needs back-transforming). District boundaries carry the ARTERIAL roads (`freewayWidth` half-width, normalized into street units like everything else) and are WIGGLY — each boundary bends with two seeded sine octaves (`cityWiggle`, amp `CITY_WIGGLE_AMP`), and district ASSIGNMENT follows the same curve so the rotated-grid frame switch always stays buried under the arterial surface; arterials also absorb the seams between differently-rotated grids, and block plateaus ramp to the shared MID-PLATEAU FREEWAY GRADE (maxBlockElevation/2, spread over `CITY_FREEWAY_RAMP_START` 14 → `CITY_FREEWAY_RAMP_END` 34u, both past the lanes so a freeway is flat across its four lanes — grade 0 with a tight ramp was rejected: every freeway read as a V trough) at arterials so elevation stays continuous across districts. Arterial markers follow the curve, oriented along its tangent (`cityWiggleSlope`). Within a district the layout is a square BLOCK GRID (`gridSize` per cell; NOT voronoi — a voronoi partition was tried and rejected as too round/organic) — each cell rolls a block index in `[0, blockCount)` (cached per cell in `getCityCell`, seeds salted by district key; a REMNANT — a cell the city's EDGE roads (belt, arterials, quays) leave fewer than `CITY_REMNANT_MIN_CORE` (8 of 25) buildable core samples of (`cellSurvey` in `roads/cityCells.ts`: 9×9 samples of the edge field by the road field's own formulas, from the walls, the arterial curves and the raw river pieces alone — no terrain, a cell is built mid-vertex) — takes the label of the full square neighbor its land lies toward, else through a remnant neighbor that has one, else `CITY_REMNANT_LABEL` (a strip of remnants is one block), so its land joins a block instead of standing alone; two steps at most, nothing recurses. This REPLACED the all-road RIM cells (−1 within gs × 0.15 of the wall, Evan's screenshots 91/92): beside every belt they left wide asphalt fills holding tiny sidewalk islands, 265 asphalt spots > 36u from any land and 436 islands under 1500u² over 8 city areas, MEASURED; 16 and 0 after); neighbors that roll the same index merge into larger polyomino blocks. (1) STREETS run along boundaries of differing-label cells, computed as boundary SEGMENTS over the full 3×3 neighborhood rather than per-cell infinite lines — segments entering/leaving the set at a cell border are always ≥ ~half a cell away, keeping the field AND the s1+s2 chamfer continuous (per-cell line constraints were tried and REJECTED: the chamfer's second constraint popped identity at cell borders, leaving jagged notches in road edges). Contiguous collinear segments along one boundary line are MERGED into single runs before being considered — otherwise a straight road along two merged same-label cells is two collinear segments and the chamfer pairs them (two pieces of the SAME road), undercutting the field and notching the sidewalk at every merged-block cell seam; (2) SHAPE FEATURES rolled per 2×2 SUPER-CELL in `getCityCell` (each replaces FOUR normal blocks) — TRIANGLE super-cells (`triangleChance`): one unique label across the super-cell (boundary streets guaranteed) with a corner-to-corner diagonal road splitting it into two large flatiron halves that always terminate in intersections; ROUNDABOUT super-cells (`roundaboutChance`): a large circular block (island) inside a ring road centered on the super-cell — member cells COPY an outward neighbor's label (`baseCityLabel`, non-recursive), so the wrap-around blocks outside the ring MERGE seamlessly with the surrounding grid (only the ring road separates them from the island); internal member boundaries whose copied labels differ become the streets radiating from the roundabout — inside the ring, boundary constraints are suppressed (the toggle hides under the ring road) so those streets tee into the ring instead of crossing the island, and the island's height is overridden to its own flat plateau (blended in under the inner ring road). The island's field is compressed ×0.75 so buildings keep a safe margin from the curved curb. Features only spawn when `superCellHasRoom` passes — the whole super-cell must be clear of the biome rim AND of the district's arterial boundaries, so no half circles or clipped diagonals at boundaries. Features are CONFINED to their own super-cell — the core no-tiny-pieces guarantee (cross-block avenue lattices were tried and REJECTED: lines crossing many blocks manufacture fragments). The biome RIM is a BELT FREEWAY — a freeway ring surrounding the whole city that the outbound arterials empty into, and it IS the city's border: its centerline sits ON the biome wall, so its inner half is the city's rim and its OUTER half is graded, dipped and painted by the shared off-city freeway code (computeVertexData step 5 + the terrain shader's `city_frag` corridor — see "Inter-city freeways and bridges"); the inter-city runs leave from its wall junctions, so a run and the belt meet as one road-network node. Inside the city it is fed into the road field with the freeway normalization + a recovery that starts at `CITY_BELT_RECOVER_NORM` (earlier than an arterial's, so blocks stand near it), at the same mid-plateau grade with the full curb dip up to the wall (the neighbor side dips by the same formula), and dressed EXACTLY like the other freeways: dashed lane lines (dash phase = the biome-wall projection — the same `along` the outside computes, so the phase is continuous across the wall) and median raised markers (enumerated by stepping the city's wall segments in warped space and INVERTING the road-noise warp by fixed-point iteration (`unwarp`); validity filtered through computeVertexData — not by biome, a marker on the centerline may fall a hair to either side of the wall; the zone-wall list holds each wall twice, endpoint-swapped, so only the canonical orientation is processed); blocks melt/chamfer against it like any road (the remnant cells it cuts join their neighbors), and street markers stay clear of the belt corridor; (3) ARTERIALS — the district boundary roads described above (there is no separate freeway lattice anymore), normalized into street units (× `roadWidth / freewayWidth`) so ONE road field drives the shader bands, curb dip, and spawn filters (arterial features just render stretched). The normalization only applies through the visual bands: past `CITY_ARTERIAL_RECOVER_NORM` (12.2 normalized, just beyond the interior-band start) the arterial constraint RECOVERS at `CITY_ARTERIAL_RECOVER_SLOPE` (steep) via a continuous max() of the two slopes — without the recovery, the squash held the field below the building-spawn threshold for ~11×roadWidth real units and left the blocks along every arterial empty; with it, buildings reach a near-normal setback (~21u) from the freeway curb. The road field is min over all constraints — pure closed-form arithmetic per vertex (cached cell lookups + a handful of line distances), no voronoi/Delaunay; a RIVER through the city adds a QUAY ROAD constraint along each bank (see the rivers paragraph). Where a city wall running ALONG a river lies in its footprint (its belt's river-side curb in the water; `wallDrownedAt`, sampled along each wall per river list, only walls within 30–50° of the river — a belt CROSSING a river is decked, not drowned), the BELT is carried on the water's straight edge as the WATERFRONT (`findWaterfrontBelt`, Evan: "the freeway perimeter of the city should continue along the edge of the river"): the drowned wall's belt distance is pushed off by `CITY_WATERFRONT_PENALTY` and a line at bank + freewayWidth from the river takes over, joined by a smooth minimum (`CITY_WATERFRONT_FILLET` 30u, a rounded corner where the belt comes out of the water); across a partly drowned wall the belt holds until its share reaches `CITY_WATERFRONT_BELT_HOLD` (0.6) and the line's push eases off by smoothstep of the share, so the two OVERLAP (fully in play from a share of 0.4 instead, a second branch of the line ran beside the first where the quay field folds: a ridge of curb teeth, the sidewalk triangles inside a plaza of screenshot 98); a wall's share fades over `CITY_WATERFRONT_FADE` (60) CENTERED on the line where its river-side curb is just dry (over 30u past that line the share rose 0.17 in 5u where a wall leaves the river at an angle, and the push tore the belt's outer edge into spikes of curb and sand beside a deck, 97; over 60u past it, dry walls beside a river mouth gave their belt way to a block's tip, 103) — pushed off together (the belt by the share, the line by the rest) both stood ~22u off midway, and the belt ended in a stub on the sand beside a separate waterfront (Evan's screenshot 90; street dead ends over 8 city areas 8 → 1) — one road, one set of belt lanes (its paint never "ends" at the river), its river-side curb on the bank; off the city the belt is measured the city's own way (`drownedBeltDistance`: pushed off a drowned wall, the waterfront line pushed away with the distance outside the wall), its outer half beside a river is the city's inner half at the wall (graded and dipped by the quay-aware field `beltQuayField`, then carved like city ground), and it keeps no paint; at the wall the city's road field is at least the belt's, falling off inward (`CITY_WALL_FIELD_FALLOFF`), so a street running into a drowned wall meets the neighbor's field. A drowned wall's waterfront fades in with its share (`CITY_WATERFRONT_ONSET`) and out before the distance cutoff, and the plateau ramps to the belt grade by the wall distance too: switched on at once these stepped the city up to 1.9u, and the city's quay terrain stood up to 2.7u off its neighbor at 51 crossings (± 12000: 684 city-wall crossings over 0.1u before, 0 after). A widened quay was tried first and REJECTED (two dash sets crossing, a plaza). Constraints are collected with their toward-road unit DIRECTIONS (world frame; rim = NaN, pairable with anything) and feed a fully PAIRWISE linear chamfer/melt: `min over pairs of (dᵢ + dⱼ + penalty) × CITY_CHAMFER_SCALE` — block corners at intersections get straight 45° cuts and fragments pinched under ~`roadWidth / scale` span become road entirely. The penalty fades in smoothly (`CITY_CHAMFER_DOT_LO/HI/PENALTY`) as the two directions align: inside a corner wedge or pinch they are ≥ 90° apart (no penalty), while a road event ACROSS the street — a T-junction stem or far-side bend — points the same way as the near road and must NOT notch the block edge (pairing s1-with-s2 without the direction test was tried and REJECTED: it bit cutouts into straight edges opposite every T-junction). Kept LINEAR and fully pairwise deliberately (a smoothstep-SCALED melt bends band contours into blobs; argmin-based pairing switches identity discontinuously). Each block index has a seeded plateau elevation in `[0, maxBlockElevation]`; elevation is bilinear plateau interpolation toward the neighbors the vertex leans into, ramping across street AND sidewalk (roadWidth + CITY_RAMP_SPAN each side) where labels differ. The city RIDES the regional base noise; flat building footing comes from the generic FLATTEN-GROUND PADS, not from the city (whole-block leveling via flood-filled polyomino anchors was built and REVERTED in favor of pads — pads work in every biome), avenues/freeways don't move plateaus (they just carve road surface across flat tops), and the road surface dips `curbHeight` below the sidewalk. `cityConfig.roadWidth` (7) is the street HALF-width (centerline → curb) and must match the band constants in the city fragment shader, which paints asphalt + gutters (0–7), curb strip (7–8), sidewalk (8–12), and plaza-concrete block interior (12+), plus fake directional shading from `vWorldNormal` so ramps/curbs read on the unlit terrain. FREEWAYS (`freewayWidth` 14 = 2× streets) are 4 LANES: raised markers stud the median, and each side splits with a white DASHED painted line at ±freewayWidth/2 — drawn from `vDistanceToFreewayCenter` (REAL-units distance to the nearest freeway centerline, arterial edge OR belt; 99999 outside the city and in JUNCTION ZONES — wherever a second freeway feature is within `freewayWidth + 10` — so paint ends cleanly before interchanges) plus `vFreewayAlong` (the dash-phase coordinate: axis coordinate for arterials, biome-wall projection via `lastWallAlong` for the belt), both plumbed VertexResult → terrain.worker → TerrainRenderer attributes → vertex.glsl varyings → city fragment shader because the shared normalized road field cannot distinguish freeway surface from street surface. CRITICAL: both varyings JUMP between adjacent vertices at wall-segment seams / axis switches / mask boundaries, and interpolation would sweep mod() into ZEBRA-STRIPE artifacts — the shader's fwidth() guards (a core GLSL ES 3.0 builtin under WebGL2 — three ≥ r158 has no `extensions.derivatives` flag to set) detect those slivers by their absurd world-space gradient and drop the paint there; do NOT paint from a phase varying without this guard. NO painted centerline — road centers get RAISED PAVEMENT MARKERS instead (paint shimmered): `objects/dressing/road-markers/RoadMarkers.tsx` (mounted in the city biome) builds one InstancedMesh of unlit yellow studs per 256u chunk, no colliders; positions come from `getCityRoadMarkers` in `utils/workers/roads/roadMarkers.ts` (via the dressing worker — `dressingWorker.ts`), which enumerates grid boundaries (owned by midpoint bounds) and lattice lines (markers on a global parameter lattice) — both duplicate-free across chunks — insets markers away from intersections, and filters per-marker by biome/river/on-road-center checks. Spawn placement uses the `roadDistanceRange` descriptor filter (also in normalized street units): buildings `[28, ∞)` / skyscrapers `[33, ∞)` (raised from 23/28 for the 1.5× width scale, building/DECISIONS.md 7) (block interiors only — densities are raised to offset the smaller candidate area; footprint spacing is the packing limiter), street lamps `[8.2, 11.8]` (the sidewalk band).
