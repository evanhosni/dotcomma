# Rocks

## How it works

Low-poly rocks over a biome and at its cave mouths. EVERY biome that mounts `<Rocks …/>` is one density field of its own, with that mount's own props; one driver draws them all.

- **Placement** ([spec.ts](spec.ts) `ROCK_PLACEMENT`, read by the server too): the `rocks` enumerator → `getRocks` ([utils/workers/rocks/rockPlacement.ts](../../../utils/workers/rocks/rockPlacement.ts)). ONE DENSITY FIELD PER BIOME THAT MOUNTS `<Rocks/>`, with that mount's own props over `ROCK_DEFAULTS` (`rockFields()` in rockPlacement.ts; the mountain takes the defaults — 900 per million sq u, footprint 5, 0.4–13.5u, keeping 12% of flat points rising to all between 10° and 32° — and the tundra's mount sets 110 / 6 / 0.4–3u / 30% / 8° → 25°). No biome is named anywhere in the rock code, sizes cubically biased small, none within 3u of a road's or freeway's paved half width, none inside a cave opening, plus every rim and strewn spot of every mouth of every cave biome's caves (a `<Caves>` in the biome) (the cave biomes from the LIVE config). Each rock belongs to the chunk holding it; its shape, half sizes (0.8–1.3 / 0.5–1.0 / 0.8–1.3 of its size), yaw, tilt (±0.35 rad), shade (#5a5753–#8e8a84) and burial (its lowest DRAWN ground, under its middle and the four ends of its width, holds 20–50% of its height) are seeded from its position. A mouth's rock is dropped where its footprint's ground rises more than a rock height across it (the hood's face); any rock whose footprint reaches over an opening is dropped. Pinned by [rocks.test.ts](../../../utils/workers/rocks/rocks.test.ts).
- **Waterfall boulders** ([utils/workers/rivers/waterfallRocks.ts](../../../utils/workers/rivers/waterfallRocks.ts), the river's rule, joined to the field rocks by the `rocks` enumerator with no biome probe): along every built river piece, one rock attempt per 14×14u of water on average where the piece falls faster than `WATERFALL_GRADE` (0.25) and per 32×32u in calm water (the odd rock at a river's shallow edge, with no spray: a burst needs a fall's grade), across 95% of the half-width, sizes 1.2–7u biased small (u²); kept only where the point is wet, not under a deck, not in a lake biome. Each rests on the carved bed (the same burial) but is LIFTED so its top breaks the sheet by max(0.6u, 70% of its half height), and dropped when that lift would hang its base more than 1.5u + 8u × grade over the ground under its centre (the steeper the fall, the whiter and more opaque its sheet). Bare (`snow` 0), wet (the darker half of the shade range). Same look helpers (`rockLookAt`, `rockFootprint`, `rockRestingY`, `rockPointOf`). The splash enumerator reads the SAME list (`getWaterfallBoulders`, with each rock's downhill direction and grade) to stand a burst of white water on every wet upstream face. Pinned by [waterfallRocks.test.ts](../../../utils/workers/rivers/waterfallRocks.test.ts).
- **Shapes** ([rockShapes.ts](rockShapes.ts), Three-free): 8 icosahedra with every corner pushed 0.72–1.12 of the radius and slid ±0.11 sideways; closed, wound outward.
- **Art** ([Rocks.tsx](Rocks.tsx)): ONE merged mesh per chunk (every lump baked in place, its shade and its `snow` as vertex attributes, rebased on the chunk's middle), flat-shaded, drawn to 1400u in the mountain wall's rock look (`dirt.png`, triplanar on the wrapped world position, tinted 0.62/0.62/0.66, each rock lighter or darker by its shade over 0.18), snow on faces whose normal rises 0.55 → 0.8 when the rock's `snow` is 1 (a field or mouth rock in a biome with the `snow` flag; never a waterfall boulder), in the terrain's own light (fake sun, night dim) written over the scene's lighting. No far sprite tier.
- **Colliders** (`ROCKS_SPEC`): exactly the drawn lump (its shape scaled per axis) as a trimesh on a body with the rock's yaw and pitch, within 60u; the server builds the same.

## How to add another

N/A: one mount covers the region. Tune the fields in `ROCK_PLACEMENT`; a new rocky biome is one more field. The waterfall boulders have no mount knob at all (both enumerators must agree on every rock): tune the constants in `waterfallRocks.ts`.
