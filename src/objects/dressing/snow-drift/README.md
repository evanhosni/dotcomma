# Snow drift

## How it works

The snow PILES over every biome whose spec says `snow: true` (the tundra and the mountain): one continuous sheet per 256u dressing chunk, so touching piles are one surface rather than separate mounds. Placement and depth are [utils/workers/snow/snowDrift.ts](../../../utils/workers/snow/snowDrift.ts) (Three-free), run by the dressing worker (the `snowDrift` enumerator).

- **Where snow lies:** an AMOUNT per point = cover (the snow biomes' share of the terrain, 0 under water or within 0.3u of it, under a deck, fading in over 4u past road, freeway and river keep-offs) × the snow line (0 below 40u of ground height, full by 100u, so none on the tundra) × (0.08 + 0.45 drift + 0.65 altitude + 0.12 lumps) × flatness (full below 40° + 15° × altitude, thinning over ±6°) − 0.32, with altitude 0 at 20u rising to 1 by 250u, drift a smooth 0–1 world field (~50–90u) and lumps a −1..1 field (~6–9u). Snow lies where the amount is positive; the cover fades out over the same 4u into a cave mouth's keep-off, so the sheet's edge runs smoothly around the opening.
- **Depth:** min(1.85u, (0.6 + 0.8 drift) × (1 + 0.5 altitude) × (1 + 0.2 lumps)) × a quarter-circle edge over the first 0.18 of amount — never over the 2u player.
- **The near sheet** ([SnowDrift.tsx](SnowDrift.tsx), default 440u, just past LOD1's ring): a 2u grid standing on the ground as LOD1 draws it ([drawnGround.ts](../../../utils/workers/underground/drawnGround.ts)), cut along the smooth curve where the amount crosses zero (interpolated along edges, 0.05u under the ground), smooth normals shared across chunk borders, the slope for the limit read across a whole 4.375u cell. Shaded as the mountain's snow ([snowMaterial.ts](snowMaterial.ts): 0.93/0.95/0.98 × (0.6 + 0.4 fake sun), night dim, no tone mapping, double-sided); each vertex sinks by its depth + 0.15u as the camera distance goes 320 → 400u. No collider.
- **The terrain's own snow, at every LOD:** the terrain worker gives every chunk a per-vertex snow depth (`terrainSnowDepthFrom`: the same rule from the vertex's height, cover and grid slope, no cave keep-off; the pile's depth, or the ground's own snow — reaching 15° steeper (softened over ±16°) and 0.08 of amount past the piles' edge, whole 0.3 of amount past its own — as a depth up to 0.35u), riding the `underwaterDepth` attribute NEGATIVE (snow never lies under water), and the terrain shader whitens the ground by it (full by 0.35u), in the snow region's drift-varied white. Entrance patches carry the same.
- **Wading and traction:** `snowMovementAt` folds the pile's depth into a capsule's movement modifiers ([physics/movementModifiers.ts](../../../physics/movementModifiers.ts)): walk speed × max(0.25, 1 − 0.42 × depth), and every slope limit (the uphill fade, the 40° slide angle) raised 8° per unit of depth up to 12°, so deep snow is slow but grippy. Every capsule — the player, the server's NPCs, local actors — samples it off its stepping thread, with its feet height (no wading more than 1.5u under or over the drawn ground: a cave under the summit), and hands it to `stepCharacter` (physics/README.md "Surface").
- **Tracks:** the near sheet dents where capsules walk ([snowTrail.ts](snowTrail.ts)): each stamp sinks the sheet within its radius by the local depth and recovers over 20s.

## How to use

- **Snow over another biome:** mount `<Snow />` in it (props override `SNOW_DEFAULTS`). It reaches every worker and the server through the domain config, and the one driver draws every biome that mounts it.
- **Tune:** the constants at the top of `snowDrift.ts`.
