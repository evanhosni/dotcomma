# Street lamps

## How it works

Lamp posts along the city sidewalks (`<StreetLamps/>`, the **density-placed** dressing template, [../README.md](../README.md)) and along both sides of every inter-city freeway run (`<FreewayLamps/>`, structured placement). One component body (`LampPosts` in [StreetLamps.tsx](StreetLamps.tsx)) draws both from a collider spec: the drawn yaw IS the spec's body yaw.

- **Placement:** the `densityPoints` enumerator with `LAMP_PLACEMENT` from [lampSpec.ts](lampSpec.ts): density 4200, footprint 14, `roadDistanceRange` [8.2, 11.8] (the sidewalk band), city biome only. Density is high on purpose, because the sidewalk band is thin and footprint spacing does the real limiting. Each lamp's yaw is a hash of its position (`lampYaw`).
- **Freeway placement:** the `freewayLamps` enumerator (`getFreewayRunLamps`, [../../../utils/workers/roads/runLamps.ts](../../../utils/workers/roads/runLamps.ts)) with `FREEWAY_LAMP_PLACEMENT`. Each side of a run has its own lattice on the run's arc length, 48u apart; the far side is offset by 24u (STAGGERED), so along the road a head stands every 24u, which is the lamp-glow grid's cell (one head per 24u texel). The lateral offset is SOLVED against the road field (the road is drawn from a meandered point, ±3u), landing the pole 1.5u past the shoulder paint (20.5u from the centerline, on the grass). Where that verge lies on the grade's cut/fill ramp (more than `maxRise` 1.5u off the road, or steeper than `maxSlope` 40°), it falls back to the shoulder paint (16.4u). The arm points at the road. Dropped: in the city; within 40u of a city wall (the belt and the run's merge mouth); within 40u of a run's end (hubs); beside a centerline with no lane paint (merge mouth, lane end before an undecked river) or on or near a deck (`underDeck` at the pole, and at the centerline ±16u); in a river's footprint (`riverKeepOff`) or a lake. The base sinks by the slope across the pole so it never floats. Power poles stand only in the city, so the two never meet. Measured over a 13×13 km area: 29 lamps per km of off-city run (the nominal 41.7, less run ends, hubs, mouths and steep cuts; about 45% on the shoulder in hilly country), 0 in the city, water or on decks, no duplicates under forward, reverse or shuffled chunk order. `runLampDebug` counts the drops by reason.
- **Art:** [lampGeometry.ts](lampGeometry.ts) merges pole, arm and head into one vertex-colored mesh. An `aLampMask` attribute limits the emissive glow to the head. The whole chunk is one draw call with one shared material, whose emissive intensity follows the day/night window-lights ramp.
- **Light:** each chunk registers its heads in the lamp-glow grid (`registerLampHeads` in [../../../lighting/lampGlow.ts](../../../lighting/lampGlow.ts)), so the pavement around them lights up at night. There are no real point lights. The chunk registry calls the returned disposer when a chunk unmounts.
- **Colliders:** `STREET_LAMPS_SPEC` and `FREEWAY_LAMPS_SPEC` in [lampSpec.ts](lampSpec.ts) (listed in [../catalog.ts](../catalog.ts)): cuboids (`LAMP_COLLIDER_PARTS`) for lamps within `LAMP_COLLIDER_DISTANCE` (60u). The server builds the same colliders from that spec.

Files: [StreetLamps.tsx](StreetLamps.tsx) (the component), [lampSpec.ts](lampSpec.ts) (Three-free sizes, colliders, placement and the collider spec, shared with the server) and [lampGeometry.ts](lampGeometry.ts).

## How to use/add

Mount `<StreetLamps />` and `<FreewayLamps />` once each, in a biome's `<Dressing>` (today: the city biome; the runs exist only between cities, so their lamps sit with their markers and decks). Props: `renderDistance` (default 440), `colliderDistance`; `<StreetLamps>` also takes the placement props `density`, `footprint`, `roadDistanceRange`, `biomeIds`, `heightRange`, `slopeRange`.

Tune the placement in `LAMP_PLACEMENT` / `FREEWAY_LAMP_PLACEMENT` ([lampSpec.ts](lampSpec.ts)), not at the mount: the server places its colliders from the spec, and a mount override logs a dev warning saying so. Tests: [../../../utils/workers/roads/runLamps.test.ts](../../../utils/workers/roads/runLamps.test.ts).
