# Underground (caves and sewers)

## How it works

Tunnels under a biome's cells: caves under the mountain, sewers under the city. They are an OVERLAY on the terrain, and part of the world rather than scenery standing on it: generation reads the ground, the terrain's openings are cut per pixel, and the player ignores the heightfields overhead while in a tunnel. The one height step they make is the cave mouth bulge. Decisions and measurements: [utils/workers/underground/DECISIONS.md](../../utils/workers/underground/DECISIONS.md).

**Generation** (Three-free, [utils/workers/underground/](../../utils/workers/underground/); every worker, the main thread and the server get the same systems, bit for bit, from the seed and config alone):

- `systems.ts`: one system per biome-grid cell whose site belongs to the biome and passes its roll (`systemAt`: caves 0.6, sewers 0.75), remembered per thread (512, the oldest half dropped), found by area (`systemsNear`, cells up to two away around the area padded by the road warp's maximum; `caveMouthsNear` for the mouths, which the bulge, the rocks and the snow ask for). While one generates (`generatingTunnels`), every height it reads leaves the bulge out, so no mouth depends on its own bulge. `clearTunnelCaches` runs on every `initCompute` (with the mouth tiles, the drawn-ground lattice and the snow cover). The cave biomes are the ones that mount `<Caves>` ([biomeFlags.ts](../../utils/workers/biomeFlags.ts): `caveBiomeIds()` reads the LIVE config, the one flag the bulge, the foliage keep-off and the rocks all read).
- `caves.ts`: an entry mouth found by walking from the cell's site out until the ground leaves the biome and back in to the first climb (60% then climb on 20–100u up the ray to the first spot whose section fits, else back at the foot), its section the shortest length descending at 0.2 that keeps 4u of rock over the passage's crown; once the trunk's floors settle each mouth's inner floor is the trunk's (so the entry and its first tube always meet), and a section then steeper than 0.35 is no way in (a steep exit makes a loop, a steep entry no cave); a direction with no fit mouth tries the next of six; an exit with chance 0.5; ONE main path, a smooth bow between the two inner ends or a straight lead to a junction and a ring (the junction visited twice on one floor); 2–4 blind branches; rooms on the trunk and at branch tips; stalactites and stalagmites rooted 2.5u into the ACTUAL rock the whole system's air probe finds. Floors aim at the line between the mouths' inner floors and dip only where the cover demands, never steeper than 0.3. Layout and decoration draw from separate seeded streams (`seededStream`, [utils/math/seededStream.ts](../../utils/math/seededStream.ts)); the curves are `paths.ts`. Each mouth's rock spots (`mouthSpots`: rim cells of a 2u grid whose ground lies outside the ragged opening with a neighbour's inside it, then strewn rocks from 0.6 of their reach in front of the opening to that reach into the mountain) are placed on demand, on the BULGED ground.
- `sewers.ts`: the district holding the cell's site, its block intersections within 4 blocks of the nearest one, a node usable on-street and a street usable where every 4u sample is and the ground keeps 4u over the pipe's crown; a THROUGH path of 5–9 streets (chance 0.5) or a LOOP (1–2 lead streets then a ring around a 1×1 to 2×2 block rectangle, else 4 more streets); 2–4 blind branches; manholes on every second main node (and a through path's far end), rooms with chance 0.35 on the others, doors with chance 0.45 on junctions where the side room clears the rest. `finishSewer` (after generation, so the ground it reads is bulged and the drawn-ground lattice may fill) rests each manhole's frame and cover on the street. `mergeLayouts` joins the systems of one district by node and street; everything drawn comes from the merged layout. `sewerMesh.ts` builds the walls as explicit level, plumb or arch faces (every vertex snapped to the shader's quantization lattice, and every box's walls, floor cells and ceiling built on ONE set of edge points, so no face's vertex lies mid-edge on another's — the shader snaps each vertex on its own, and a T-junction is a crack) and the liquid's quads.
- `tunnelField.ts`: the AIR as one signed field (negative in the air): pieces of a path union plainly, paths of a system meet in a smooth junction (2.5u), formations are subtracted, systems overlap; a function of the point alone (a piece counts only inside its bounds padded by `FIELD_REACH`, wall noise only within `NOISE_SHELL` of a wall), so any subset of the pieces holding the air reads the same bits. `boxIsRock` is the sound lower bound the mesher skips bricks by.
- `mouthBulge.ts`: the ONE height step. `computeVertexData` adds the mound, the hood and the ledge around every mouth of the biomes that mount `<Caves>` (step 6, before the pads), from mouths fetched first (step 0, per 128u tile), never on roads, rivers or water. It also tells the foliage and snow workers where nothing grows (`insideCaveKeepOff`: the widest outline + 0.9 radii + 3u around a section).
- ENTRANCES have no seam (`entrance.ts`, `chunkMesh.ts`): the cave mesher meshes "air above the drawn ground, or in the tunnel" as ONE isosurface, so ground and walls share vertices. Every triangle whose centroid lies in a mouth's FOOTPRINT (the capsule along its section, its widest outline + `FOOTPRINT_PAD`) is the PATCH: its ground vertices snapped exactly onto the ground as LOD1 draws it (`drawnGround.ts`), with the terrain's normals and per-vertex fields, drawn with the terrain's own program (its ENTRANCE variant), whose color fades into the walls' rock over `PATCH_ROCK_FADE` under the ground. The walls past the footprint are drawn with the same rock formula (`world/shaders/tunnelRock.ts`), so where they meet the patch nothing shows. The terrain discards inside the footprint less `FOOTPRINT_BAND`, and only up to `MOUTH_HOLE_CAP_RADII` opening radii + `MOUTH_HOLE_CAP_PAD` over the section's floor (the opening's crown and hood): the mountain above a steep entrance stays. On the GROUND the patch keeps exactly the complement, per pixel, from the same hole uniforms (`mouthCutsHere`), plus the band, across which the patch and the terrain are the same ground; its WALL pixels are never cut (past the footprint's end and above the cut's ceiling nothing stands behind them). Outside the footprints the ground's own triangles are dropped (the terrain is there).

**Chunks** (`chunkMesh.ts`, on the tunnel worker `utils/workers/underground.worker.ts`, its own thread, which ALWAYS answers): per 256u streaming chunk ([world/chunks/](../chunks/README.md)) and per style and biome, the cave walls by surface nets over the air field, per 16u brick (`surfaceNets.ts`: crack-free across bricks and chunks; bricks no air can reach or provably all rock are skipped), or the sewer walls as explicit faces cut where they stand over the drawn ground (every vertex snapped to the shader's quantization lattice, so the faces meeting at a join are one point after the shader's own snap), one trimesh collider per 16u brick column of exactly those triangles, per-vertex color and daylight (full near a mouth's axis point, gone by 45u; sewers 1.5 → 14u from a manhole; plus light within 6u under the ground), the entrance patch, the mouths whose footprint reaches the chunk (with every streaming chunk each spans), and for sewers the liquid and the doors standing in the chunk. Buffers transfer zero-copy.

**Client** ([index.tsx](index.tsx): the `<Caves>` and `<Sewers>` declarations and their drivers): the WORLD's streaming chunk lifecycle ([world/chunks/](../chunks/README.md)) over the tunnel worker ([worker.ts](worker.ts)), its assets and its collider gating, the door and cover animation and the pick. What a chunk draws is [meshes.ts](meshes.ts): the walls ([material.ts](material.ts): the rock formula or the sewers' vertex colors, scaled by daylight down to 6%, plus a headlamp), the liquid (glossy green with drifting streaks, lit by the player's own light alone; swum in thick: `SEWER_LIQUID_VISCOSITY`), THE door ([objects/doors](../../objects/doors/README.md)) with its frame (the jambs and lintel inside the opening, the leaf a hair smaller than the clear opening between them), and each manhole's cover, frame ring (draped on the sampled street) and ladder. Everything standing in a tunnel (doors, rungs, the liquid) is lit as the deep walls; the cover and frame lie on the street, lit by the sky. Colliders: the brick columns, a slab per ladder (the player stops against it and climbs by holding jump) and a slab per closed door (rebuilt on a toggle), within 64u. Sewers pick the door or manhole under the screen center every 3 frames (a door leaf within 6u; from above a cover's disc within 6u, from below the hole within 25u, through the shaft's foot when under the box ceiling); a click toggles it; a cover slides between its two rests over 0.8s.

**The registry** ([registry.ts](registry.ts)): every loaded chunk in the player's `TunnelSpace` ([utils/workers/underground/tunnelSpace.ts](../../utils/workers/underground/tunnelSpace.ts): the probe, the floor, the rock escape, the manhole columns, the liquid, the mouth cut; one compiled field per 32u cell, cleared when the loaded set or a manhole's state changes — each server NPC asks the server's), plus the client's skirt masks and mouths, and the ONE per-frame driver every mounted tunnel feature calls (`driveUnderground`, run once per frame whoever calls first): the entrance fades, the LOD1 sites, the terrain's openings (every 10 frames, or at once when the loaded set or a fade changed: the 16 nearest holes and the 20 nearest skirt masks within 400u, [world/shaders/terrainHoles.ts](../shaders/terrainHoles.ts)) and the far mouths. The last chunk unloading clears them. The manholes' and doors' open state is [toggles.ts](toggles.ts): per client, per session, keyed by id.

**Cave LOD.** Two stages, cross-faded. NEAR, the entrance (terrain cut, patch, walls) stands on LOD1's drawn ground: a mouth whose footprint chunks are all loaded becomes a LOD1 SITE (`setLod1Sites`, world/terrain/lodQuadtree.ts), so the terrain draws the LOD1 chunks within 24u of it at LOD1 out to `LOD1_SITE_DISTANCE` (840u), and its fade tracks that LOD1 swap's own progress under it (`lod1ShareAt`, TerrainRenderer.tsx; at most one LOD fade per LOD fade): the ground change and the entrance happen together. Cave chunks load to `CAVE_RENDER_DISTANCE` (840 + 256u), their walls and patches invisible until a mouth of theirs has fade > 0 (chunks with no mouth: within 420u). FAR, out to `FAR_MOUTH_DISTANCE` (1260u, dithering out from 1008u), a mouth is an IMPOSTOR ([farMouths.ts](farMouths.ts)): one instanced disc per mouth (≤ 64, nearest first) centred on the tube's axis, 1.35 outline radii wide, facing out, pulled toward the camera by 0.6% of its distance, 75% opaque, a dark core fading to a lighter rim, strength = 1 − the entrance fade (complementary screen-door pixels), fetched from the worker within 1260 + 420u and re-fetched after 420u of travel (`createTravelRefetch` + `selectNearest`, utils/nearbyPool.ts, shared with the city lights), projected through `projectChunkLocal` on the fetch origin like every chunk-relative program.

**The player** ([player/undergroundMode.ts](../../player/undergroundMode.ts): the shared rules of [physics/tunnelRules.ts](../../physics/tunnelRules.ts) on `playerTunnels`; every server NPC runs the same): in a tunnel once a point 0.3u over the feet is in its air, out once the field there has exceeded 0.5u for 10 frames running (with no tunnel chunk loaded the probe is `known: false` and the state holds); the ground safety nets stay off for 1.5s after. Inside, the terrain heightfield is ignored while that point is in the tunnel's air (overhead, or the ground over an opening, which the drawing cuts) and is ground again across a mouth's opening plane; in a manhole's column, always. The field closes each mouth with rock past its opening plane; in front of an opening that rock is fictitious (open air over the ground: `inFrontOfOpeningAt`, `tunnelField.ts inFrontOfMouthOpening`), and neither rock backstop below acts there. An open manhole catches a player stepping onto it (snapped to its centre, held until 2u under the street), a held jump climbs at 7u/s and, near the street, sets the player down at the exit; a closed one is solid street from 0.5u under it and stops the climb 0.15u under its cover. Inside, the green liquid is the water. Both ground safety nets are suspended inside; the tunnel has its own two. The capsule's axis is never in the rock BESIDE it: the probe point in rock with a sideways field gradient (a wall whose collider was absent for a frame: at sprint speed one frame puts the axis 0.16u into the wall, and from inside, the walls hold nothing in any direction) is pushed out along the gradient the same frame, to the radius plus 0.1u of air (`escapeFromRockAt`, `tunnelField.ts rockEscapeAt`). Feet sunk 0.15u into the rock UNDER a floor (an upright gradient) are set on the floor's surface over it (`airAboveAt`, bisected to the rock's edge: a coarse air sample set them 0.15u under a sewer floor, where the sewage's swim sank them again, a bounce). So a missing or tunnelled collider never leaves the player in the rock. Pinned on the real column colliders in server/test/underground.test.ts.

## How to use

- A biome has tunnels because `<Caves chance renderDistance colliderDistance>` (default 0.6, 1096 (840 + 256), 64) or `<Sewers …>` (default 0.75, 300, 64) is mounted IN it, and nothing else. Each runs ONE chunk lifecycle per biome that mounts it — unlike the other dressing, whose single driver covers every mount — because tunnel chunks are built per biome cell and never overlap. The server and every worker read the same mounts through the domain config ([params.ts](../../utils/workers/underground/params.ts)).
- Their PARTS are children: `<Rooms chance>` in the sewers (a door into a side room per junction, 0.45) and `<Rooms trunkChance tipChance>` in the caves (0.5 on the trunk, 0.35 per branch tip); without one, no side rooms (they draw last from the layout stream: the rest of the system is unchanged). An `<Actor actor={…} />` inside `<Rooms>` stands one of that actor on each room's floor (`utils/workers/featurePlacement.ts`), simulated by the server like any NPC.
- The bulge, the rock and snow dressing and the foliage keep-off find the cave biomes from the config the tree resolves to (`caveBiomeIds()`).
- A new style is a generator in `utils/workers/underground/`, a branch in `systemAt` and `buildUndergroundChunk`, and a component here.

**Spring caves** (`caves.ts planSpring` / `finishLoneSpring` / `finishConnectedSpring` / `finishPairedSpring`, `springs.ts`, `systems.ts springSystemsAt`): where a river ends in the rock (rivers/riverPieceRules.ts placeSprings) a mouth opens on its last piece end, its floor just under the water, leading through a gently descending flooded passage to a POOL ROOM — the river's source. Two springs that are each other's nearest (over this cell and the eight around it) with their leads ending within 170u CONNECT instead, whatever their levels: one hall between them with one sloping sheet of water, one river through the mountain and out of both mouths; the hall belongs to the pair's first member by key. Only a spring with no partner ends in a pool. The system's `liquid` polygons (`SpringWater`: points, depth, flow) are drawn by the TERRAIN's own water sheet — `springWater.ts springWaterAt` is step 8 of `computeVertexData`, giving a vertex under the mountain the cave water's level, depth and flow, and the sheet dives under that water within `SPRING_WATER_MARGIN` of it — so the river runs into the cave as one mesh; the chunk's liquid triangles are only swum in (`liquidLevelAt`). Decisions: utils/workers/underground/DECISIONS.md "Spring caves".

**The dark.** Deep in a tunnel the walls keep 2.8% of their lit color, tinted blue; the player's PERSONAL LIGHT (lighting/personalLight.ts, driven by the Player from `entranceDaylightAt` here) lights their own surroundings once they have stood in the dark for a second, and nobody else's. The SKY stays the sky from inside (through a mouth or an open manhole); what keeps it out of the walls is the closed seams and the end caps (below). The day/night cycle reaches a tunnel only through `tunnelShade`'s DAYLIT term (world/shaders/tunnelRock.ts): a mouth or a manhole's surroundings dim with the ground at night, the deep ambient and the personal light never do, and no tunnel program dims its output a second time.

**The far end.** A sewer runs past its render distance (300u, 256u chunks), and past the last loaded chunk the tube was open onto the world: the terrain's culled backfaces, far ground and sky. Every sewer chunk carries END CAPS (`UndergroundChunk.caps`, built by `sewerPlaneSections` in `sewerMesh.ts` and fanned in `chunkMesh.ts`): on each of its four borders, one face across every tube the border plane cuts — a pipe's exact oblique section grown 15% about its axis, a rectangle over the chord of every node box (with its shaft, up to the street) and side room, 0.5u past them — keyed by the dressing chunk across the border ("minX,minZ"), in the walls' color and daylight, facing into the chunk. The frame loop draws a cap only while its neighbour chunk is not loaded (`isCellLoaded`), so a loaded join never shows a wall; the overshoot into the rock hides behind the walls. The caves have no caps (their 1096u render distance and winding tubes never showed an end). The devmode FLASHLIGHT (`DEV_TOGGLES`) is a cone along the camera's view in the same tunnel shaders, local too.

**NPCs** go where the player goes: the server's physics world holds a `tunnels` chunk layer around every NPC (server/src/game/physics/tunnels.ts: the client's own chunk builder, kept as pieces, entrances, liquid and the colliders of [colliderBodies.ts](../../utils/workers/underground/colliderBodies.ts)) and a `TunnelSpace`, and each NPC body runs the player's rules on it (physics/tunnelRules.ts): it walks the caves and sewers, falls down an open manhole (it never climbs out), and one spawned on a side room's floor stays on it. Manholes and doors are the SERVER's state per domain ([toggles.ts](toggles.ts) mirrors `init.tunnelsOpen` and every `tunnel:state`; a click sends `tunnel:toggle`); their ids carry the city district (a node key is local to its district's lattice). Pinned in server/test/npcTunnels.test.ts.

Not done: nothing keeps spawned surface actors (buildings, NPCs) off cave openings (foliage and snow are kept off).
