# Character movement

## How it works

[index.ts](index.ts) is the one movement resolver for every kinematic capsule: the local [Player](../player) and every ground NPC on the server ([server/src/game/physics/bodies/Walker.ts](../../server/src/game/physics/bodies/Walker.ts)). It imports only Rapier types, so the server can bundle it.

The caller passes a direction, speed and jump flag; `stepCharacter` sets the body's next kinematic translation and fills a `CharacterStepResult`. Each step:

- **Ground probe**: a downward ray with slope-adaptive reach decides walkable support (Rapier's grounded flag alone is unreliable).
- **Slopes**: full speed below `SLOPE_SOFT_START`, uphill input fades out toward `SLOPE_SOFT_END`, sliding down the fall line above `SLOPE_SLIDE_ANGLE`. Both responses need leaky persistence (`SLOPE_ENGAGE_DELAY`, `SLIDE_ENGAGE_DELAY`). A collider tagged a FULL-SPEED SLOPE skips the uphill fade and is climbed at the walk speed ALONG its surface (the solve alone keeps only cos² of an uphill walk). The slide still applies above `SLOPE_SLIDE_ANGLE`. Building ramps are tagged; terrain never is.
- **Gravity / jump**: `GRAVITY`, `JUMP_IMPULSE`, `TERMINAL_VELOCITY`; gravity resets only on ray-confirmed support, fall speed is capped near ground (`NEAR_GROUND_FALL_SPEED_CAP`); `vyOverride` lets an NPC (or a manhole climb) set its own vertical speed.
- **Swimming** (`input.swim`, a `SwimInput`): under a surface the capsule sinks slowly, each jump PRESS is a stroke up (`SWIM_STROKE_IMPULSE`, bled back to the sink; holding does nothing, so rising takes repeated presses), a press near the surface breaches. Its `liquid` (`"water"` or the sewers' `"sewage"`) picks its walk and slope multipliers (applied from the feet under the surface, wading or swimming: see Surface) and its `LIQUID_VISCOSITY` (water 1, sewage 2.5), which divides every vertical swim speed and acceleration and multiplies the drag. A SWIMMER has no slope limits (`NO_SLOPE_LIMIT`: no slide, no uphill fade, the Rapier climb wall lifted), and its sink stops on the bed (support within the contact offset along the bed's normal); a WADER keeps the ground's slopes, scaled by the liquid.
- **Surface** ([movement-modifiers/index.ts](movement-modifiers/index.ts)): every capsule feels its ground through `input.surface`, a `MovementModifiers` — a walk `speedFactor` and a `slopeFactor` (traction: multiplies the uphill fade's start and end and the slide angle, which also moves the Rapier controller's climb wall; the slide angle never passes `MAX_SLIDE_ANGLE`, 55°). EVERY kind of ground is one entry of `SURFACE_MOVEMENT` ([movement-modifiers/constants.ts](movement-modifiers/constants.ts)), a `speed` and a `slope` multiplier, constant over the whole surface: snow, water, sewage. The ground sources are folded by `surfaceModifiersAt(x, z)` (generation/index.ts); the only one today is the snow (generation/snow/index.ts `snowMovementAt`: the "snow" entry wherever the pile is at least `SNOW_WADE_DEPTH` deep, and the `snowDepth` the snow trail reads). The liquids are not sampled: the stepping side knows them (`input.swim`), and `stepCharacter` multiplies their entry in while the feet are under the surface. The CALLER samples it on a worker through ONE `createSurfaceSampler(interval, fetch)` (every `interval` steps one request, never two in flight, the last answer kept in `.modifiers`): the Player and a local kinematic actor every 6 frames with `fetchSurfaceModifiers` (the dressing worker), a server Walker every 10 ticks with `PhysicsWorld.surfaceModifiersAt` (the chunk generator), each with the capsule's FEET height: a source on the ground's surface (snow) ignores a capsule more than 1.5u under or over the drawn ground (a walker in a cave under a snowy summit was wading). Never evaluate it on the stepping thread: it runs the height pipeline, and a cold mountain cell generates its caves there (DECISIONS.md). A new kind = one `SURFACE_MOVEMENT` entry (+ its `SurfaceKind`) and one `combineMovementModifiers(out, kind)` call where its source is sampled.
- **Terrain**: the terrain heightfields are tagged at creation (`markTerrainCollider`, world/terrain/chunk/utils.ts). A step with `ignoreTerrain` leaves them out of the ground probe and the sweep — the only way the terrain is ever excluded: a player in a tunnel under them (player/hooks.ts `useUndergroundMode` decides when, by the rules in [tunnel-rules/index.ts](tunnel-rules/index.ts)).
- **Anti-tunneling**: the solve is substepped to `MAX_SUBSTEP_DISTANCE` with `CONTROLLER_CONTACT_OFFSET`.

## How to make a sloped object walkable at full speed

A FULL-SPEED SLOPE is climbed with no uphill fade. It applies to every capsule, because all of them step through `stepCharacter`: the player, client-local actors (`objects/actors/model/hooks.ts`) and every server NPC (`server/src/game/physics/bodies/Walker.ts`, beebles included). Tag the collider where it is created, by one of three routes:

- **Solid dressing (client AND server):** set `fullSpeedSlope: true` on the `ColliderPart` or `ColliderMesh` in the feature's Three-free spec. `world/chunks/hooks.ts` (client) and `server/src/game/physics/bodies/index.ts` (server) both tag what they build from it. Use this for anything NPCs must walk.
- **A declarative (R3F) collider:** pass its ref to `useFullSpeedSlope(ref)` ([physics/hooks.ts](hooks.ts)). `RampCuboid` in `objects/actors/building/Building.tsx` is the example.
- **Any other imperative collider:** call `markFullSpeedSlope(collider)` on the collider `createCollider` returned, on each side that builds it.

There is no untagging: the tags are a WeakSet of collider objects, so a removed collider drops out by itself. Keep the slope under `SLOPE_SLIDE_ANGLE` (40°) or capsules still slide. A collider only the client builds (building interiors) is only ever walked by the player and client-local actors; server NPCs never meet it.

## How to add another

N/A — one shared resolver; a new capsule uses `createCharacter`, `createStepResult`, `stepCharacter` per frame and `disposeCharacter` on unmount (NPCs just set `movement: "ground"` in their spec).

Tune `GRAVITY`, `JUMP_IMPULSE`, `TERMINAL_VELOCITY` and the `SLOPE_*` / `SLIDE_*` constants; they affect the player and every server NPC.
