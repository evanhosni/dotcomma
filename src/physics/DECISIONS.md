# Player movement — decisions

> Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md (Oct 2026) to keep it under the context limit. Section names quoted in the text ("Blending", "Rivers", …) refer to the other decision docs listed in CLAUDE.md under "Where the docs are".

**Player movement** (`physics/characterMovement.ts`, shared with every server NPC — tune it there, nowhere else)
- **Slopes are three bands, not a hard stop**: ≤ 25° full speed; 25–45° the UPHILL input component scales to 0 (contour/downhill unaffected); > 40° (`SLOPE_SLIDE_ANGLE`, also the Rapier climb wall) the capsule slides down the fall line. The slide angle is decoupled from the slowdown band and set from a MEASURED slope distribution (LOD1 collider resolution, grassland): natural terrain tops out at ~47° (median 25.6°, p99 42.6°, > 45° is 0.17% of the area, > 50° none), and capsule-solid steep spots number 257 per 600×600 patch at 40° vs 15 at 45°. 55° was tried and REJECTED as unreachable (sliding stopped happening at all) — re-measure before retuning. Responses need PERSISTENCE (slowdown 0.1s, slide 0.15s) via LEAKY timers (decay 2×, saturating at 2× the delay): pad edges and heightfield slivers are tiny steep faces that would otherwise flicker them every step.
- **Support is RAY-GATED**: gravity/jump support = a downward ray hit within snap reach of walkable (≤ 40°) ground. Rapier's `computedGrounded()` alone flickers false on heightfields (gravity then integrated without reset toward terminal velocity and the KCC deflected the huge sweep along even 2–3° slopes into permanent drift, "always sliding no matter how flat") AND holds true on steep faces (a capsule crept up a 44° slope at ~2.6u/s on the server). The ray's reach is SLOPE-ADAPTIVE (the surface sits 1/cos θ below the capsule center; a fixed feet-length reach missed the ground beyond ~55°, so the slide never engaged and jumping stayed possible exactly there).
- **Fall-through defenses — do not weaken**: fall speed is clamped near ANY ground (a terminal-velocity scrape at a glancing angle punches through triangles); the KCC solve is SUBSTEPPED to ≤ ~capsule-radius sweeps (`propagateModifiedBodyPositionsToColliders` between substeps); `CONTROLLER_CONTACT_OFFSET` 0.08 (0.02 allowed a sweep starting inside a triangle to pass through); `setNormalNudgeFactor(0.02)` recovers shallow penetrations. The AUTHORITATIVE backstop is the Player's: every 3 frames the capsule is compared against the ANALYTIC terrain height (the source the heightfields are built from) and lifted if > 2u below — the physics ground is per-chunk heightfields swapped during LOD changes, so sweep hardening alone can never close every timing hole. STUCK ESCAPE is its shallow sibling (wedged slightly inside the surface, every sweep ~0): sustained input with ~no movement for ~0.2s triggers a height check and a lift. Any below-surface test must CONFIRM with the padded height (`resolveEmbeddedSurface`): pads excavate, so raw height is not a lower bound (see "Flatten-ground pads").

**Full-speed slopes (Oct 2026).** Building ramps steepened to ≈38° (building/DECISIONS.md 10). The uphill fade, tuned on terrain, cut a climb there to ≈27%, and Evan asked for ramps (and any sloped object, later) to be exempt for EVERY capsule. A collider is tagged in a WeakSet of collider objects (`markFullSpeedSlope`; ray hits return the world's own object, so identity is safe, and a removed collider drops out with no untagging). The probe reads the tag, and `steerWalk` skips the fade there. Skipping the fade alone was not full speed: the solve slides an uphill walk up the slope plane, keeping cos² of its horizontal. Measured on the server: 2.98u/s horizontal at 38° against a 5u/s walk. So a tagged slope scales the uphill input by 1/cos(angle), and the speed ALONG the surface is the walk speed (3.69u/s horizontal; an untagged ramp: 0.75u/s). Pinned in server/test/physics.test.ts ("full-speed slopes").

**The surface sample is an INPUT to the step, sampled off the stepping thread (Oct 2026).** The first snow wading called the depth lookup from inside `stepCharacter`. The lookup reads the snow cover lattice through `computeVertexData`, so on the stepping thread it ran the height pipeline at the capsule every frame, and the pipeline's cave-mouth step GENERATES a mountain cell's caves the first time a thread touches it. MEASURED sprinting through the mountains (Chrome and Firefox, real GPU, CPU profile): one 217–314ms frame at each new cell, 232ms of it `generateCave` (findEntry/findExit) under the step; the city and tundra runs (no caves) showed ≤63ms. The server's tick had already met the same cost (chunkGenerator.ts: a cold `computeVertexData` is 254–385ms there) and answers height queries on its worker, so the sample follows that route on both sides — today as the movement modifiers of the next entry: the Player sampling `fetchSurfaceModifiers` on the dressing worker every 6 frames, a Walker `PhysicsWorld.surfaceModifiersAt` on the chunk generator every 10 ticks, each through the one `createSurfaceSampler`, keeping the last answer. The lag is a few frames of the previous answer at a drift's edge. REJECTED: a non-generating variant of the sample (the lattice heights and the mouth keep-off both need the cell's caves, so it would be a second height path), and generating on a TaskQueue (`generateCave` is one synchronous ~100–250ms call; the queue can only split BETWEEN tasks).

**Movement modifiers: the ground's drag and traction are ONE object every capsule reads (Oct 2026).** Evan asked that snow slow the walk more than the 0.22/u factor did AND let a capsule climb steeper (deep snow packs under the feet), both growing with the depth, held "in some context, as many things in the game will effect these", for every character controller. `physics/movementModifiers.ts` is that context: `{ speedFactor, slopeBonus, snowDepth }`, speed factors multiplying and slope bonuses adding (clamped to ±15°: the ground probe stops reading ground as ground past ~60°, and 40° + 15° stays under it), filled by `surfaceModifiersAt` from every source in turn (the snow today). The step applies `slopeBonus` to all three slope limits — the uphill fade's start and end and the slide angle — and moves the Rapier controller's climb/slide wall with the slide angle (two wasm calls, only when it changed by more than 1e-4 rad). The snow's numbers: speed × max(0.25, 1 − 0.42 × depth) (the deepest 1.85u pile: a quarter walk), +8° per unit of depth up to +12°. It rides the same off-thread sampling as the snow factor it replaces (the entry above; the local kinematic actors now sample too), and the one input also carries the snow depth the Player stamps the snow trail with. REJECTED: a registry of source functions the step calls itself (the step must stay sample-free, see above) and a per-source list in CharacterInput (the step would combine on every frame what the worker can combine once).

**The modifiers test caught the copy inverted (Oct 2026, the rethink).** The pinned server test added by the rebuild (`server/test/physics.test.ts` "movement modifiers": a walker wades at its surface's speed factor and its slide angle moves by the bonus) failed on first run with both walkers travelling 5.28u: every sampler (the Player, `kinematicMover.tsx`, the server Walker) called `copyMovementModifiers(answer, own)`, copying its stale neutral modifiers INTO the fresh answer, so no sampled snow depth ever reached a step. The calls are `copyMovementModifiers(own, answer)` (`into`, `from`, like `combineMovementModifiers`); the client's pinned rule test (`snow.test.ts`) could not see it, since it checks `surfaceModifiersAt` itself.

**Rising out of embedded ground (Oct 2026).** Evan, bouncing at (5891.5, -13.4, -9210.3) in a sewer channel, the camera reading putting the feet 0.25u UNDER the drawn floor. Replayed over the real column colliders (server/test/underground.test.ts), standing or walking, with the sewage and the tunnel rules: nothing bounced, but a capsule placed 0.25u under the floor stayed there for good — the controller never pushes a shape out of a one-sided trimesh it already intersects, and the ground ray from the centre still reads the floor as support, so neither gravity nor the sweep moves it; the tunnel's sunk rule starts at 0.45u. The resolver now recovers it: the ground probe reports how far the surface stands above where the capsule would REST on it (the cylinder's half plus radius / nY for the plane's tilt, less the ray's distance; a standing capsule reads the contact offset's -0.08), and over 0.03u the step adds up to 0.1u a step of rise to the desired movement, so the capsule climbs out in a few frames and the snap never pulls it back (no gap under it until it is out). Every capsule, client and server; a strengthened defence, not a weakened one. Pinned: 0.25u under the channel floor, with and without the swim, the feet are on the floor within a second with no reversal. What sank it is not reproduced; the collider-less frame of the stuck entry (underground/DECISIONS.md) is the known way, since fixed.
