# Player movement — decisions

> Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md (Oct 2026) to keep it under the context limit. Section names quoted in the text ("Blending", "Rivers", …) refer to the other decision docs listed in CLAUDE.md under "Where the docs are".

**Player movement** (`physics/characterMovement.ts`, shared with every server NPC — tune it there, nowhere else)
- **Slopes are three bands, not a hard stop**: ≤ 25° full speed; 25–45° the UPHILL input component scales to 0 (contour/downhill unaffected); > 40° (`SLOPE_SLIDE_ANGLE`, also the Rapier climb wall) the capsule slides down the fall line. The slide angle is decoupled from the slowdown band and set from a MEASURED slope distribution (LOD1 collider resolution, grassland): natural terrain tops out at ~47° (median 25.6°, p99 42.6°, > 45° is 0.17% of the area, > 50° none), and capsule-solid steep spots number 257 per 600×600 patch at 40° vs 15 at 45°. 55° was tried and REJECTED as unreachable (sliding stopped happening at all) — re-measure before retuning. Responses need PERSISTENCE (slowdown 0.1s, slide 0.15s) via LEAKY timers (decay 2×, saturating at 2× the delay): pad edges and heightfield slivers are tiny steep faces that would otherwise flicker them every step.
- **Support is RAY-GATED**: gravity/jump support = a downward ray hit within snap reach of walkable (≤ 40°) ground. Rapier's `computedGrounded()` alone flickers false on heightfields (gravity then integrated without reset toward terminal velocity and the KCC deflected the huge sweep along even 2–3° slopes into permanent drift, "always sliding no matter how flat") AND holds true on steep faces (a capsule crept up a 44° slope at ~2.6u/s on the server). The ray's reach is SLOPE-ADAPTIVE (the surface sits 1/cos θ below the capsule center; a fixed feet-length reach missed the ground beyond ~55°, so the slide never engaged and jumping stayed possible exactly there).
- **Fall-through defenses — do not weaken**: fall speed is clamped near ANY ground (a terminal-velocity scrape at a glancing angle punches through triangles); the KCC solve is SUBSTEPPED to ≤ ~capsule-radius sweeps (`propagateModifiedBodyPositionsToColliders` between substeps); `CONTROLLER_CONTACT_OFFSET` 0.08 (0.02 allowed a sweep starting inside a triangle to pass through); `setNormalNudgeFactor(0.02)` recovers shallow penetrations. The AUTHORITATIVE backstop is the Player's: every 3 frames the capsule is compared against the ANALYTIC terrain height (the source the heightfields are built from) and lifted if > 2u below — the physics ground is per-chunk heightfields swapped during LOD changes, so sweep hardening alone can never close every timing hole. STUCK ESCAPE is its shallow sibling (wedged slightly inside the surface, every sweep ~0): sustained input with ~no movement for ~0.2s triggers a height check and a lift. Any below-surface test must CONFIRM with the padded height (`resolveEmbeddedSurface`): pads excavate, so raw height is not a lower bound (see "Flatten-ground pads").
